# Options & Volatility Strategies

A quantitative research project exploring options markets, volatility modelling, derivatives analytics, and systematic options strategies.

## Overview

This repository focuses on developing a deeper understanding of volatility-driven strategies through mathematical modelling, quantitative analysis, and computational research.

It covers the complete research spectrum, from volatility forecasting and options pricing to strategy development, backtesting, and portfolio construction.

## Key Areas of Focus

- **Volatility Modelling:** Statistical models, volatility forecasting, and regime analysis.
- **Options Analytics:** Option pricing, Greeks, volatility surfaces, and derivatives data.
- **Strategy Research:** Systematic options strategies, volatility trading, and dispersion strategies.
- **Quantitative Backtesting:** Strategy evaluation, performance analysis, and risk assessment.
- **Machine Learning:** Applying ML techniques to volatility modelling and quantitative research.
- **Portfolio Construction:** Risk-aware volatility portfolios and institutional approaches.

## Objective

The objective is to combine financial mathematics, statistical modelling, programming, and quantitative research to study volatility-driven opportunities in options markets.

The project emphasizes rigorous analysis, reproducible experimentation, and a research-oriented approach to understanding derivatives and volatility strategies.
